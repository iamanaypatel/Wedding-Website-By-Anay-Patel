How It Began

Our Love Story

From a rainy Tuesday afternoon in a cramped San Francisco bookstore to a sun-drenched cliffside in Big Sur, every moment has led us here.

Est. October 2021

"I knew from the first conversation over lukewarm espresso that my life had quietly shifted forever."

Aria was searching for an out-of-print architecture monograph; Julian happened to be holding the last copy on the shelf. What started as a polite negotiation over who needed the book more turned into a four-hour dinner down the street at a neighborhood trattoria.

Five years, three apartments, countless road trips up the Pacific Coast Highway, and one very spoiled golden retriever named Olive later, we are overjoyed to gather our favorite people in the world in Napa Valley to celebrate our marriage.

San Francisco International (SFO) and Oakland International (OAK) are both approximately 75–90 minutes south of Napa Valley by car.

Charles M. Schulz–Sonoma County Airport (STS) offers regional flights and is an easy 40-minute drive to St. Helena.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code for the wedding?

Our ceremony and reception dress code is Black Tie Optional. Tuxedos or dark suits are encouraged for gentlemen, and floor-length gowns, midi dresses, or dressy cocktail attire for ladies. Because the ceremony takes place on a manicured lawn, we recommend block heels or wedges!

Are children invited to the celebration?

While we adore your little ones, our Saturday wedding ceremony and reception will be an adults-only celebration (21+) so that all parents can relax and enjoy the evening. Children are warmly welcome at Friday’s casual Welcome Dinner, and we are happy to connect you with trusted local childcare services in Napa.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Due to intimate venue capacity at Villa Aurelia, we can only accommodate guests formally named on your invitation envelope. If you received a plus-one, it will be clearly noted alongside your name when you RSVP.

Will the events be indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony and cocktail hour will be held outdoors in the gardens, where September daytime temperatures typically hover around a pleasant 78°F (25°C). Dinner and dancing will take place inside the climate-controlled Glass Conservatory. We suggest bringing a light wrap for the evening breeze.
